Biomedical researcher focused on molecular mechanisms of disease.

Research

I study the functional and molecular implications of cancer-associated mutations in a critical tumor suppressor. I use biochemical and molecular biology approaches to investigate the molecular landscape of common hotspot mutations observed in endometrial cancers.

  • (Conference proceeding) Kafle CM, Bell DW. Investigation of the mechanism of PADI2 regulation by the FBXW7 tumor suppressor. Clinical Cancer Research, 2024. DOI

Prior to this, I studied Adenovirus-induced cellular DNA damage response, cell cycle, and DNA replication. Our findings showed that adenovirus induction of DDR is required for activation of G2/M checkpoint kinases in E4-mutant infected cells. We also showed that E4-mutant infected cells are arrested at a phase characterized by either 4N or greater-than-4N DNA content, preventing progression through mitosis.

  • Kafle CM, Anderson AY, Prakash A, Swedik S, Bridge E. An Adenovirus early region 4 deletion mutant induces G2/M arrest via ATM activation and reduces mitotic marker expression. Virology, 2021. PMID: 34626906

Earlier Work

I also investigated the efficacy of artemisinin–lumefantrine–based combination therapy (ACT) for the treatment of Plasmodium falciparum malaria in Nepal. In this multi-institutional collaborative study, we conducted a therapeutic efficacy assessment among falciparum malaria patients treated with ACT in regions characterized by high mobility of migrant workers.

  • Ghimire P, Rijal KR, Kafle CM, et al. Efficacy of artemether-lumefantrine for treating Plasmodium falciparum malaria in Nepal. Tropical Diseases, Travel Medicine and Vaccines, 2018. PMID: 30123520
  • Kafle CM, Ghimire S, Dhakal P, Shrestha U. Identification of parasites in soil samples of vegetable fields in Bhaktapur District. Tribhuvan University Journal of Microbiology, 2014.
